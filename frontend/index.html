Cleveland Heart Disease Feature Dictionary

Comprehensive description of all 14 clinical parameters, medical significance, and encoding schemas.

# Feature Code Clinical Name Type Values / Range Medical Significance
1 age Age Continuous 29 - 77 years Cardiovascular risk increases progressively with advancing age.
2 sex Biological Sex Binary 0 = Female, 1 = Male Men historically exhibit earlier onset of coronary artery disease.
3 cp Chest Pain Type Categorical 1: Typical, 2: Atypical, 3: Non-anginal, 4: Asymptomatic Asymptomatic angina (type 4) frequently signals silent coronary ischemia.
4 trestbps Resting Blood Pressure Continuous 94 - 200 mm Hg Hypertension accelerates endothelial vascular injury.
5 chol Serum Cholesterol Continuous 126 - 564 mg/dL Elevated LDL contributes to atherosclerotic plaque accumulation.
6 fbs Fasting Blood Sugar Binary 0: ≤ 120, 1: > 120 mg/dL Surrogate marker for diabetes, a recognized cardiovascular multiplier.
7 restecg Resting Electrocardiogram Categorical 0: Normal, 1: ST-T wave, 2: LV hypertrophy Identifies pre-existing ventricular strain or conduction anomalies.
8 thalach Max Heart Rate Achieved Continuous 71 - 202 bpm Chronotropic incompetence (inability to reach target HR) indicates cardiac stress.
9 exang Exercise Induced Angina Binary 0 = No, 1 = Yes Indicates insufficient myocardial perfusion under physiological demand.
10 oldpeak ST Depression Continuous 0.0 - 6.2 mm ST segment depression during stress test indicates subendocardial ischemia.
11 slope Peak Exercise ST Slope Categorical 1: Upsloping, 2: Flat, 3: Downsloping Flat or downsloping ST indicates significant coronary occlusion.
12 ca Major Vessels Colored Discrete 0 - 3 vessels Fluoroscopy assessment; more opacified vessels indicate widespread disease.
13 thal Thalassemia Scintigraphy Categorical 3 = Normal, 6 = Fixed defect, 7 = Reversible Nuclear perfusion defect; reversible defects identify salvageable ischemic myocardium.
🎯 target Heart Disease Diagnosis Binary Target 0 = Absence, 1 = Presence Primary prediction label (> 50% vessel diameter narrowing).
