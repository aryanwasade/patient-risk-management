Data Patterns & Visual Insights

Exploratory charts showing how health tests, habits, and symptoms connect with heart disease.

We explored 303 patient records to uncover what health signs most strongly warn of heart disease. The data clearly shows: patients with silent chest pain (type 4), exercise-induced heart wave dips (oldpeak), and blocked blood vessels (ca ≥ 1) are much more likely to have heart disease.

Heart Health Feature Guide (Student-Friendly)

A simple plain-English guide to all 14 data columns, what they test, and why they matter.

# Data Column Plain Name Data Type Range / Options What It Means (Simple Words)
1 age Age Number 29 - 77 years Heart disease risk naturally increases as people get older.
2 sex Gender Category 0 = Female, 1 = Male Men in this historical study showed earlier signs of heart issues.
3 cp Chest Pain Type Category (1-4) 1: Typical, 2: Unusual, 3: Non-heart, 4: No pain Describes how chest pain feels. People with no pain (type 4) often had hidden severe heart issues.
4 trestbps Resting Blood Pressure Number 94 - 200 mm Hg High blood pressure puts extra strain and wear on heart blood vessels.
5 chol Cholesterol Level Number 126 - 564 mg/dL Too much cholesterol can build up fatty deposits inside blood vessels.
6 fbs Fasting Blood Sugar Yes / No 0 = Normal, 1 = Sugar High (>120 mg/dL) Checks for high blood sugar or diabetes, which adds stress to the heart.
7 restecg Resting Heart Monitor (ECG) Category (0-2) 0: Normal, 1: Unusual wave, 2: Thick heart muscle A quick electrical test that records heart waves while lying quietly.
8 thalach Maximum Heart Rate Number 71 - 202 bpm Highest heart rate during exercise. A heart that cannot beat fast enough under stress may be weak.
9 exang Chest Pain From Exercise Yes / No 0 = No, 1 = Yes Did exercise cause chest pain? If yes, the heart muscle may not be getting enough oxygen.
10 oldpeak Heart Stress Wave Dip Number 0.0 - 6.2 mm How much the ECG wave drops during hard exercise. Bigger dips mean the heart is under stress.
11 slope Heart Wave Slope During Exercise Category (1-3) 1: Sloping up (healthy), 2: Flat (warning), 3: Sloping down The direction the heart wave travels during peak exercise. Flat or downward lines indicate heart strain.
12 ca Blocked Blood Vessels Count Number (0-3) 0 - 3 vessels Number of major heart arteries showing blockages on an X-ray scan. More blocks = higher risk.
13 thal Heart Blood Flow Scan Category 3 = Normal flow, 6 = Old scar, 7 = Temporary issue A scan showing whether blood flows smoothly to all areas of the heart muscle.
🎯 target Heart Disease Result Goal Label 0 = Healthy / No Disease, 1 = Heart Disease Detected What our machine learning model is trained to predict.
